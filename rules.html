Agreements

The No-Ledge Tree is a space for shedding. To maintain the clarity of this sanctuary, we operate under these silent agreements:

1. Shed, Do Not Accumulate

This is not a place for debate or the imposition of new beliefs. Every contribution is an act of release—a shedding of a thought, bias, or ledge that has lost its utility. If you are here to convince others, you are in the wrong grove.

2. Practice the Three Movements

When you engage, choose your intent carefully. Use Mirror to acknowledge, Echo to reflect the underlying assumption, and Dissolve to provide the friction necessary for growth. These tools are for investigation, not for weaponization.

3. Maintain the Silence

Do not pollute the ecosystem with noise. Spam, ego-driven commentary, and hostility are incompatible with the spirit of unlearning. If you bring them here, they will be pruned to protect the growth of others.

4. Accept the Flow

The tree evolves constantly. By planting a seed, you accept that your contribution is part of a larger, living structure. Thoughts may be pruned, perspectives may shift, and the ledge you stand on today may be dissolved tomorrow.
