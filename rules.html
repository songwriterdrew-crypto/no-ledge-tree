The Agreements of the Grove

The No-Ledge Tree is a space for shedding. To keep the atmosphere restorative, we operate under these silent agreements:

1. Focus on the Unlearning

This is not a place for debate or the imposition of new beliefs. Every contribution should be an act of release—a shedding of a thought, bias, or burden that no longer serves you.

2. Cultivate the Silence

Do not pollute the grove with noise. Spam, hate speech, and harassment are incompatible with the spirit of unlearning. If you bring them here, they will be pruned.

3. Own Your Reflection

The thoughts you plant here are reflections of your own journey. Respect the privacy and the journey of others. We are all here to empty our hands, not to fill them.

4. Embrace Impermanence

The tree grows and changes. Thoughts may be pruned, and the grove will evolve. By planting a seed, you accept that your contribution is part of a larger, living collective that is meant to be light, not heavy.
